How NURV Shares Work
Ground Floor — Commercial
Fractional Ownership
Own Part of NURV Mall
Without Buying the Whole Shop
NURV Mall shares let you invest directly in ground-floor commercial real estate in Al Shorouk, starting from a fraction of the cost of a full unit — with the management burden handled entirely by the operator.
What Is a Share?
34 Equal Shares Per Unit
Every unit at NURV Mall is divided into 34 equal shares, so no single share can exceed roughly 3% of the unit's value.
A Documented Legal Interest
A share is a registered interest (حصة مشاعة) in the physical unit — not a symbolic stake or a fund unit.
Transparent, Mechanical Pricing
Share price is calculated directly from the unit's published rate per square metre — gross area × rate ÷ 34 shares. No hidden markups.
Flexible Position Size
Hold 1 to 4 shares in a single unit, and up to 10 shares in total across the project.
Full Unit Ownership vs. a NURV Share
Buying a Whole Unit A NURV Share
Entry Point The full unit value — EGP 6.1M to EGP 19.2M for ground floor units A single share, from roughly EGP 179,000
Capital Required 100% of one unit's value, financed as one large commitment Invest exactly what fits your budget — 1 to 4 shares per unit
Management You handle leasing, maintenance, and tenant relations yourself Professionally managed by the mall operator — no landlord duties
Vacancy Risk Carried entirely by you if your tenant leaves Spread across a professionally managed pool of tenants and units
Rental Income 100% of one unit's rent, if it's leased A proportional share of rental income, from Year 3
Capital Appreciation Full unit value appreciation, tied to a single asset Proportional appreciation on your share, from Year 1
Diversification All capital concentrated in one unit Spread up to 10 shares across multiple units
Liquidity Reselling a whole commercial unit is a slower, higher-friction transaction Smaller share size makes resale, transfer, or inheritance more accessible
How It Works
1
Choose a unit and share count. Pick from any of the 11 ground floor units and decide how many shares — 1 to 4 — you'd like to hold in it.
2
Pay a deposit (optional) and finance the balance. Instalments run up to 48 months, interest-free, calculated on your share investment only — never the full unit value.
3
Receive your registered share contract. Your ownership is documented and held in your name.
4
Collect proportional rental income from Year 3. Capital appreciation applies to your share from Year 1, regardless of leasing status.
